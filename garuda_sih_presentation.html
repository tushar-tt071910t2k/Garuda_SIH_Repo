🌍 SIH Problem Statement & Motivation

Conventional autonomous-driving systems are developed around structured roads with clear lane markings, predictable traffic flow, standardised signage, and controlled intersections. Indian roads frequently contain heterogeneous road users and less predictable behaviour — cars, buses, trucks, autos, two-wheelers, bicycles, pedestrians, pushcarts, and animals sharing the same road space.

📊 Scale of the Problem

India records ~1.5 lakh road fatalities/year — world's highest. 70% of national highways pass through unstructured corridors. Without India-native AV intelligence, mass deployment of autonomous vehicles is impossible and dangerous.

Anticipated Judge Questions — Prepared Answers (from Project Documentation)
Q: Why MATLAB instead of Python / CARLA / ROS?
MATLAB provides an integrated environment for scenario generation, vehicle modeling, control, visualization, and deep-learning experimentation. This reduced integration overhead and allowed rapid testing of five targeted Indian-road cases without managing separate ROS/Python/simulation tool chains.
Q: How do you handle roads without lane markings?
The system does not depend exclusively on lane lines. It uses free-space / waypoint-based navigation combined with DeepLabv3+ semantic road segmentation to estimate drivable regions and road boundaries — making it inherently suitable for unmarked village and dense-market roads.
Q: What if YOLO misses a pedestrian or cattle?
A deterministic safety layer (CollisionAvoidance.m) remains active at all times. The design uses learned perception as an enhancement while retaining a geometric safety fallback capable of commanding braking when a hazard enters the protected region — regardless of DL confidence.
Q: Why is ReLU used in the CNN?
ReLU: f(x) = max(0,x). Introduces non-linearity while remaining computationally simple. Supports effective gradient flow for positive activations. Well-suited to the convolutional feature-extraction pipeline for visual steering/speed regression. Used after each conv block and hidden FC layer.
Q: How do you prove the system works?
The master scenario runner executes all five environments and records objective metrics: completion rate, minimum clearance, braking events, speed, and latency. Visual logs and a performance dashboard provide additional evidence. All metrics traceable to MetricsLogger.m logs.
Q: Are the models actually trained?
Answer precisely from latest training logs: Identify dataset version, number of training/validation samples, model version, training completion status, and held-out evaluation metrics (mAP for YOLO, IoU/Dice for DeepLab, MAE for CNN). Do not describe a planned model as fully trained unless final artifact confirms it.
